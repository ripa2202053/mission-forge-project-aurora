# NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026 — Section 05: Data Used
> **Official Submission Fill-up Guide for Project Aurora**  
> *Ready to copy & paste directly into the NASA Space Apps submission form.*

Each entry below is formatted with the exact 3 fields requested by the portal:
1. **Dataset name**
2. **Link**
3. **How you used it** *(Strictly one concise line matching what judges see in your demo)*

---

### Entry 01: Mars 2020 Perseverance Rover Science & Spectroscopy
* **Dataset name:**  
  `NASA PDS Mars 2020 Perseverance PIXL & SuperCam Spectroscopy Archive`
* **Link:**  
  `https://pds-geosciences.wustl.edu/missions/mars2020/`
* **How you used it:**  
  `Used for authentic rock core sample mineralogy, 2.2 µm clay absorption bands, and Jezero Delta mudstone spectra during rover drilling.`

---

### Entry 02: Mars Reconnaissance Orbiter (MRO) High-Resolution Terrain & Imagery
* **Dataset name:**  
  `NASA PDS MRO HiRISE Stereo Digital Terrain Models & CRISM Spectral Cubes`
* **Link:**  
  `https://hirise.lpl.arizona.edu/`
* **How you used it:**  
  `Used to reconstruct 3D surface topography for Jezero Crater delta scarps, geological ridge sheltering from dust storms, and landing hazards.`

---

### Entry 03: Lunar Reconnaissance Orbiter (LRO) Polar Volatiles & Thermals
* **Dataset name:**  
  `NASA PDS LRO Lunar Exploration Neutron Detector (LEND) & Diviner Radiometer (DLRE)`
* **Link:**  
  `https://pds-geosciences.wustl.edu/missions/lro/`
* **How you used it:**  
  `Used to simulate Shackleton Crater's 27 K (-246°C) cold-traps, permanent shadow battery drainage, and polar hydrogen water-ice deposits.`

---

### Entry 04: Moon Mineralogy Mapper (M3) Chandrayaan-1 / NASA SMD
* **Dataset name:**  
  `NASA PDS Moon Mineralogy Mapper (M3) Spectral Reflectance Data Archive`
* **Link:**  
  `https://pds-imaging.jpl.nasa.gov/data/m3/`
* **How you used it:**  
  `Used for 1.3 µm, 1.5 µm, and 2.0 µm near-infrared absorption signatures identifying exposed surface water ice crystals in the Science Codex.`

---

### Entry 05: Dawn Mission Archive (Asteroid 4 Vesta Protoplanet)
* **Dataset name:**  
  `NASA PDS Dawn Visible and Infrared Spectrometer (VIR) & Framing Camera (FC2)`
* **Link:**  
  `https://pds-atmospheres.nmsu.edu/data_and_services/atmospheres_data/DAWN/vir.html`
* **How you used it:**  
  `Used for Rheasilvia impact basin 3D morphology, 0.93 µm and 1.98 µm pyroxene absorption minima, and HAMO/LAMO orbital mapping dynamics.`

---

### Entry 06: Galileo Orbiter & Europa Clipper Magnetometry and Spectroscopy
* **Dataset name:**  
  `NASA PDS Galileo Magnetometer (MAG) & Europa Clipper MISE Spectrometer Data`
* **Link:**  
  `https://pds-ppi.igpp.ucla.edu/mission/Galileo/`
* **How you used it:**  
  `Used to model Europa's 428 nT induced subsurface saltwater dipole, Jovian radiation belt flux, and hydrated sulfate salts across chaos terrain.`

---

### Entry 07: NASA Earthdata GIBS Satellite Fleet (MODIS & CALIPSO)
* **Dataset name:**  
  `NASA Earthdata GIBS MODIS Sea Surface Temperature & CALIPSO CALIOP Lidar Profiles`
* **Link:**  
  `https://earthdata.nasa.gov/`
* **How you used it:**  
  `Used to render global Earth ocean chlorophyll patterns, atmospheric cloud layers, and simulate Sentinel-7 low Earth orbit observation passes.`

---

### Entry 08: NASA JPL Horizons On-Line Solar System Ephemeris API
* **Dataset name:**  
  `NASA JPL SSD Horizons On-Line Solar System Ephemeris System API`
* **Link:**  
  `https://ssd.jpl.nasa.gov/api/horizons.api`
* **How you used it:**  
  `Used to pull real-time spacecraft trajectory vectors, Cartesian positions, velocities, and true interplanetary range measurements.`

---

### Entry 09: NASA JPL Small-Body Mission Design Tool API
* **Dataset name:**  
  `NASA JPL Small-Body Database Mission Design & Porkchop Calculation API (mdesign.api)`
* **Link:**  
  `https://ssd-api.jpl.nasa.gov/doc/mdesign.html`
* **How you used it:**  
  `Used for calculating optimal ballistic transfer windows, C3 launch energy, hyperbolic excess velocity (v-infinity), and delta-v requirements.`

---

### Entry 10: NOAA Space Weather Prediction Center & NASA SOHO
* **Dataset name:**  
  `NOAA SWPC / NASA Space Weather Solar Proton & Geomagnetic Activity Stream`
* **Link:**  
  `https://services.swpc.noaa.gov/`
* **How you used it:**  
  `Used to simulate live solar storms, coronal mass ejection (CME) shockwaves, and in-flight communication blackout events.`

---

### Entry 11: NASA 3D Resources & Spacecraft Asset Archive
* **Dataset name:**  
  `NASA 3D Resources Official 3D Model Catalog (Perseverance, Ingenuity, Deep Space 1, Dawn, ISS)`
* **Link:**  
  `https://nasa3d.arc.nasa.gov/`
* **How you used it:**  
  `Used for authentic 3D spacecraft, rover, drone, and ground station geometries rendered in the interactive WebGL hangar and flight scenes.`

---

### Entry 12: Custom Open-Source Pipeline: NASA Mission Data API
* **Dataset name:**  
  `NASA Mission Data API Microservice (Aggregated NASA / JPL / PDS Rest Pipeline)`
* **Link:**  
  `https://nasa-mission-data-api.vercel.app/`
* **How you used it:**  
  `Used as a custom-built CORS-free edge microservice and offline cache delivering authentic NASA telemetry and planetary parameters to the game.`
